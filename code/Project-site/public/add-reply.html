Add a Reply

  1. Log in and select a dorm.
  2. Click the post you want to reply to in Dorm posts.
  3. Enter your reply in Write a reply.
  4. Click Reply.
  5. Your reply appears under Replies to selected post, and the reply field clears.

Select a post first. Blank replies are rejected. The app attempts to save after submitting a reply; check the status area for a save error.
