Post a Message

  1. Log in and select the dorm where you want to post.
  2. Enter your message in Write a post for your dorm.
  3. Click Post message.
  4. Your post appears in the feed with your username and posting time. The app selects the new post and clears the text area.

Blank messages are rejected. Posting requires a logged-in account and a selected dorm. The app attempts to save after posting; check the status area for a save error.
